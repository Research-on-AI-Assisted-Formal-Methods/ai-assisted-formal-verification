# Robust Formal Specifications


1. MutDafny: A Mutation-Based Approach to Assess Dafny Specifications

Quick idea: introduces Dafny mutation-testing tool “MutDafny,” which deliberately injects bugs into verified programs to uncover weak or incomplete formal specifications

Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15403



2. Inferring multiple helper Dafny assertions with LLMs


Idea: introduces 'DAISY' a tool to infer missing helper assertions in Dafny programs, making proof writing a bit easier.
